Hi, I’m Sarab – a designer with a deep interest in people, behavior and the small details that make a big difference.

My background in industrial design, architecture and service has taught me how to balance structure with empathy – and how to design with real users in mind. I’m especially drawn to accessibility design, interaction design and UI, where clear, thoughtful solutions can genuinely help people in their everyday lives.

Outside of design, I enjoy photography and experimenting in the kitchen – trying new recipes is one of my favorite ways to stay creative.
